In 2026, Dr.Bohl opened the Vienna Opera Ball, launched its first product with WEIN&CO, released a third single and expanded into advertising with the founding of Bohl Entertainment.

  1. 30 January 2026Paulus / TV

    Fakt oder Fake appearance with Tina Ritschl and Alexandra Maritza Wachter.

  2. 8 February 2026Paulus / Radio

    Radio Salzburg Café interview, focusing on his career, SOLO and the approaching Opera Ball opening.

  3. 12 February 2026Paulus / Society

    Opens the 68th Vienna Opera Ball. Paulus and his partner Léa join the first row of debutants and take part in the formal opening.

  4. 1 April 2026Paulus / Campaign

    BIPA April Fool campaign: BI BOHL. BI BIPA. BI CURLY. presents a fictional Dr.Bohl curling shampoo promising "Schneckerl wie der Doktor."

  5. 17 April 2026Paulus / TV

    Return to Fakt oder Fake, appearing with JJ and Barbara Karlich in the season's later run.

  6. Late April 2026Brand / Product

    Launch of BOHL LITER with WEIN&CO. The wine designed for white spritzers is presented at Vienna's Naschmarkt and released through WEIN&CO shops and online.

  7. 5 May 2026Paulus / Music

    Third official single: SCHNITZEL (all night long).

  8. 7 May 2026Paulus / Music video

    Official SCHNITZEL video rollout. The song is publicly revealed as having reached the final 20 from more than 600 Austrian submissions for Eurovision 2026.

  9. 17 May 2026Business / Campaign

    Bohl Entertainment enters the advertising market. The newly founded company and Wirz develop Hyundai's Marko for President social-first campaign around Marko Arnautović; its initial content approaches 3.5 million organic views before expanding into a broader 360-degree campaign.

  10. 17 June 2026Paulus / Music and football

    Marko for President WM anthem. Paulus releases a humorous song and video featuring Arnautović during Austria's World Cup campaign.

  11. 17 July 2026Duo / Podcast

    Bohlmobil episode 93: Mondsee. The continuing podcast shows that the original brother-duo format remains active alongside Paulus's solo career.

  12. 6 August 2026Paulus / Stage

    SOLO continues as a touring program, including a sold-out Theater im Park performance, with further dates scheduled into late 2026.

In 2025, Paulus stepped forward as a solo stage performer and became a national television personality. Dancing Stars, Frühstück bei mir, his first music releases, Disney dubbing and the ROMY red carpet expanded Dr.Bohl far beyond social comedy.

  1. 11 February 2025Paulus / Stage

    Premiere of SOLO at the Wiener Stadtsaal. It is Paulus's first stage program without Benjamin and centres on dating, Tinder encounters and personal stories.

  2. 14 March 2025Paulus / TV

    Dancing Stars live competition begins. Paulus is partnered with professional dancer Catharina Malek for the 16th ORF season.

  3. 18 March 2025Paulus / TV interview

    Guest on Willkommen Österreich, appearing alongside André Heller and Ursula Strauss while promoting SOLO and discussing his developing television career.

  4. 23 May 2025Paulus / TV

    Second place in the Dancing Stars final. Paulus and Catharina reach the final and finish behind Aaron Karl and Kateryna Mizera.

  5. 30 May 2025Paulus / TV

    70 Jahre Fernsehen – Die emotionalsten Momente. Paulus joins Katharina Straßer and Barbara Stöckl for the opening edition of ORF's anniversary series.

  6. 15 June 2025Paulus / Radio interview

    Frühstück bei mir with Claudia Stöckl. Paulus discusses his path from law graduate to full-time comedian, his family influence, Dancing Stars and the move from a school cellar to major sold-out venues.

  7. 29 July 2025Paulus / Music

    First official single: DU DUMMER. Apple Music lists the one-track release under the Dr.Bohl name.

  8. 17 August 2025Paulus / Music

    Second single: WEISSER SPRITZER. The song extends the Dr.Bohl identity into comic Austrian pop.

  9. 26 November 2025Paulus / Film

    First dubbing role in Disney's Zoomania 2. Paulus voices two iguanas in the German-language version, including an iguana bartender speaking in Viennese dialect.

  10. 28 November 2025Paulus / Awards and interviews

    KURIER ROMY red-carpet coverage in Kitzbühel. Dr.Bohl conducts comedic celebrity interviews with the yellow spatula, later assembled as Red Carpet Schabernack ROMY 2025.

2024 brought Dr.Bohl into national broadcasting: an official Opernball assignment, recurring ORF appearances, a three-part television broadcast of the first stage show and the interview format BOHL FRAGT.

  1. February 2024Duo / Society content

    First official Vienna Opera Ball content assignment. Paulus later recalled that Dr.Bohl had been invited as the event's first official content creators to produce comedic coverage inside the ball.

  2. From March 2024Paulus / TV

    Entry into the ORF comedy-panel circuit. Paulus begins appearing on Was gibt es Neues? and subsequently becomes a recurring member of the show's wider rotating panel.

  3. 26 August 2024Duo / Streaming

    Dr.Bohl – Live! arrives on ORF ON, giving the original stage program a national streaming release.

  4. 27 August 2024Duo / TV

    ORF 1 broadcast begins. DIE.NACHT presents the first stage program in three weekly parts — the duo's full cabaret television debut.

  5. September–October 2024Paulus / Interview series

    BOHL FRAGT launches. Paulus conducts intentionally non-political interviews with Werner Kogler, Beate Meinl-Reisinger and Andreas Babler during the Austrian election period. The extended interviews are also released as a podcast.

ANABOHLIKA marked Dr.Bohl's arrival in established Austrian cabaret. By the end of 2023, the brothers had moved from being radio guests to hosting their own three-day Ö3 special.

  1. 16 January 2023Duo / Stage

    Premiere of ANABOHLIKA at the sold-out Wiener Stadtsaal — the second Dr.Bohl stage program.

  2. February 2023Duo / Award

    Bohlmobil reaches No. 9 at the Ö3 Podcast Award, completing a progression from No. 20 to No. 19 and then into the Top 10.

  3. 20 March 2023Duo / Radio

    Ö3 guest takeover: the brothers select and discuss their personal "Top-Scheiben" in Treffpunkt Gästeliste, including their associations with Udo Jürgens, Elvis Presley, Dr. Alban and Wanda.

  4. 2 June 2023Duo / Industry

    How to Bohl! at the Ö3 Podcast Festival. They present a case study about becoming and working as comedians.

  5. 17 November 2023Paulus / TV

    ORF prime-time appearance in Hier spielt die Musik. Paulus competes alongside Anna Tropper-Lener in the musical entertainment show.

  6. 26–28 December 2023Duo / Radio

    First radio-hosting assignment: Ordination Dr.Bohl airs on Ö3 for three afternoons between Christmas and New Year, with Paulus and Benjamin hosting from 15:00 to 18:00.

In 2020, Dr.Bohl moved from social media to the stage and into podcasting. Two years later, the project had expanded into television presenting, scripted fiction and national brand events.

  1. January 2020Duo / Stage

    World premiere of Dr.Bohl – Live!. Their first stage program is performed for roughly 120 invited friends in the cellar of their former school.

  2. 16 April 2020Duo / Podcast

    Bohlmobil launches. Episode 1, "Perchtoldsdorf – Kalksburg," establishes the mobile podcast format: the brothers talking while travelling through Vienna and its surroundings.

  3. February 2021Duo / Award

    First Ö3 Podcast Award placement: Bohlmobil reaches No. 20 in the inaugural Austrian ranking.

  4. 23 February 2022Duo / Award

    Bohlmobil rises to No. 19 at the second Ö3 Podcast Award.

  5. June 2022Duo / Brand event

    Milka relaunch performance. Dr.Bohl makes a surprise appearance at Milka's major brand-relaunch event in Vienna's Filmquartier.

  6. 29 August 2022Paulus / TV

    Television-hosting breakthrough: Paulus presents the first episode of PULS 4's unconventional street-and-fitness quiz Zack, Prack, Quiz.

  7. 2022Duo / Fiction

    Tiafe Typen sitcom pilot. The independently produced pilot expands the Dr.Bohl characters into a scripted fictional format and includes performers such as Max Ortner and Gery Seidl.

What began in 2016 as a birthday video - with a yellow spatula standing in for a microphone - became a recurring comedy format in 2017. In 2018, "Studenten in den Sommerferien" gave Dr.Bohl its first viral breakthrough.

  1. 2016Duo

    The prototype birthday video. Paulus wanted to make an unconventional video for his best friend's 20th birthday. Attempts to interview strangers with the now-iconic yellow spatula largely failed, so Paulus played several invented characters himself. The response inspired the brothers to continue the format.

  2. 2017Duo

    Regular Dr.Bohl videos begin. The official site identifies 2017 as the year the brothers started regularly translating their costumes, characters and deliberately shallow jokes into video.

  3. 2018Duo

    First viral breakthrough: the clip "Studenten in den Sommerferien" spreads widely on Facebook, taking the project beyond their immediate circle for the first time.
