Main selling points:
- Feels like a real app, not a webpage
  - Single-page site - every section is one continuous experience, no full page reloads between "pages"
  - Built mobile-first, so it works and feels natural on a phone first, then scales up to desktop
  - Native app behavior: the back button closes menus/overlays instead of leaving the site, just like a real app
- Performance
  - Perfect 100/100 Lighthouse score across all four categories: Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO
  - Every image is exported in multiple resolutions (via srcset/<picture>), so a phone downloads a small, phone-sized image and a large monitor downloads a sharp, full-size one - nobody downloads more than they need
  - Content is only built/loaded when it's actually needed (e.g. the tour dates list, discography embeds, and about-page text are only assembled the moment someone opens that section) - keeps the initial load light and fast
- Design
  - One unified visual style and interaction language across the whole site - every subpage, card, and menu shares the same look and feel instead of feeling bolted together
  - Fully custom-built and handcrafted - not a template or page builder, so the design is unique to Dr.Bohl
- Content that updates itself
  - The ticket/tour dates page pulls live from a Google Sheet - so tour dates can be updated by simply editing a spreadsheet, no code or developer needed, and the site reflects changes automatically
- Visual polish
  - Photos and title-card images have been individually retouched or custom-composed for the site, not just dropped in as-is
